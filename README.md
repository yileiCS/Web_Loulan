[Website of Loulan](https://yileics.github.io/Web_Loulan/)
🏜️🐫

This a web‑based platform that integrates drone aerial videos, historical documentaries, ancient and contemporary cultural works, detailed images of artefacts unearthed, site photographs, an interactive map showing Loulan’s location along Silk Road routes, and rich descriptive texts. This project aims to raise awareness of heritage protection and climate change response, inspire active public participation in conservation efforts, and explore sustainable protection and utilization strategies together.
